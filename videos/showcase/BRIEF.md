## Intent

A short, silent tour of SuperDictate for the top of the GitHub README. A
visitor should see, in about twenty seconds, what the app does and how it
looks: words appearing as you speak, the Liquid Glass capsule, the other
skins, the languages, and the name to remember.

## Assets

- Real app UI, rendered frame by frame from `SuperDictate.exe --snapshot` (a tour
  mode): the capsule building a sentence, Liquid Glass over light and dark
  windows, every skin, the Languages and Capsule settings pages.

## Customizations

- Five scenes: Dictate, Glass, Skins, Languages, Close.
- v2: a launch-film treatment, in the style of big companies: a camera per shot (macro, pull-back, push-in, 3D cards and a picker drum), whip pans and a zoom-through between shots, kinetic type.

## Notes

- Silent: no music, no voice. Captions on screen carry the words.
- Deliverables: a GIF embedded in README.md and README.ru.md, and the MP4
  committed next to it and linked.
- Only real UI from the app; no invented features or claims.
