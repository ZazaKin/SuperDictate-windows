# SuperDictate

Dictation for Windows and Mac: speak, and the words are typed where the cursor
is, while a capsule on screen shows what's heard.

## Dictation

**Dictation**:
One recording, from the shortcut press to the typed text.
_Avoid_: Session, recording (for the whole thing)

**Dictation session**:
The rules a dictation follows: what each key press does, when a recording
counts, when it's dropped, and what the capsule says.
_Avoid_: Controller, dictation model

**Tap**:
A press too short to be speech (under a third of a second); it is not a
dictation.

**Cancel**:
Dropping a dictation with Escape, or with another key during a hold; nothing
is typed.

**Silence limit**:
A minute without speech, which finishes a dictation by itself and never presses
Enter or Return.

**Live preview**:
The draft of the words shown in the capsule while speaking; the whole recording
is transcribed again when the dictation finishes.
_Avoid_: Live text, live transcript

## The capsule

**Capsule**:
The small pill on screen that shows a dictation: listening, processing, the
live preview, or a short notice.
_Avoid_: Overlay, pill, HUD

**Skin**:
One of the capsule's looks, such as Midnight or Liquid Glass.
_Avoid_: Theme

**Liquid Glass**:
The skin made of glass: Apple's own on macOS 26, the screen behind bent and
blurred on Windows, light or dark with what's behind it.

**Live glass**:
Liquid Glass showing the real screen behind it on Windows; off, it is painted.
Where Windows can't keep the capsule out of screen copies (Windows 10), it shows
the screen as it was when the capsule came in.

**Sample**:
The capsule shown on screen with example words while the Capsule settings are
open; dictation waits while it shows.
_Avoid_: Preview, stage

**Notice**:
A short message the capsule shows for a moment, such as "Cancelled".

## Settings

**Settings draft**:
The changes made in the Windows settings window and not yet saved.
_Avoid_: Pending settings, unsaved state

**Save**:
Writing a settings draft into the settings in use; only what was changed in
the window is written.
