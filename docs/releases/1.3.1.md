# SuperDictate 1.3.1

Fixes for the capsule from 1.3.0, a way to turn the voice meter off, and a
Halftone skin that is its own voice meter.

Downloads:

- **Windows:** `SuperDictate-Setup-1.3.1.exe`. Installed copies offer the
  update on a click (**Settings › Support › Check for updates**).
- **Mac:** `SuperDictate-macOS-1.3.1.zip`, for Apple Silicon Macs with macOS 14
  Sonoma or newer.

You can verify both files against `SHA256SUMS.txt`.

## What's new

- **Voice meter: Off.** A new choice on the Capsule page leaves only the words.
- **Halftone is its own voice meter.** Its pixel wave takes the color you pick
  for the voice meter and rises and falls with your voice. Choosing Halftone
  turns the meter off; turn it back on if you want both.
- **Windows: the capsule grows smoothly.** As your words arrive, it widens in
  one continuous motion instead of a small jolt per word.

## Fixes

- If you opened the Capsule settings during a dictation, the capsule
  disappeared when the dictation ended and you couldn't see your changes. It
  now comes back as the preview as soon as the dictation ends.
- **Chrome:** the ribbons now cross the whole capsule however wide it grows,
  instead of bunching up in the middle.
- **Windows:** Halftone, Mosaic and Mesa always fill the whole capsule; after
  it grew, their picture could stop short of the right end.

## Known limitations

- The Windows installer isn't signed with a certificate yet, so SmartScreen
  asks for confirmation: click **More info** › **Run anyway**.
- The Mac app isn't notarized by Apple yet. The first time, right-click
  SuperDictate in Applications, choose **Open**, then **Open** again.
- Windows: 64-bit Intel and AMD processors only. Mac: Apple Silicon only.
