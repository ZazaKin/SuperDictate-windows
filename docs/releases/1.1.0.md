# SuperDictate 1.1.0

Your words as you speak them, a capsule you can make your own, and twenty
languages. This release also brings the first SuperDictate for Mac.

Downloads:

- **Windows:** `SuperDictate-Setup-1.1.0.exe`. Installed copies offer the
  update on a click (**Settings › Support › Check for updates**).
- **Mac:** `SuperDictate-macOS-1.1.0.zip`, for Apple Silicon Macs with macOS 14
  Sonoma or newer.

You can verify both files against `SHA256SUMS.txt`.

## What's new

- **Live preview.** Your words build up in the capsule as you talk. Each word
  rises in out of a blur, and the newest ones brighten as they settle. When
  you finish, the whole recording is transcribed again, so the typed text can
  be a little better than the draft.
- **Silence limit.** A minute without speech ends the dictation by itself and
  types what you said, without pressing Return.
- **The capsule, your way.**
  - Twelve skins: Midnight, Graphite, Paper, Glass, Aurora, Telegram, High
    Contrast, Neon, Ocean, Sunset, Forest and Frost.
  - An accent color, size, opacity, a bars, wave or pulse voice meter, and the
    recording time if you want it.
  - Put it in any corner, or click **Move…** and drag it anywhere. It snaps to
    the edges and the center. Next to the left or right edge, it grows away
    from that edge as your words appear.
  - It can follow the screen you're working on, or stay on the main one.
  - While the Capsule settings page is open, the real capsule shows on screen,
    and every change appears on it at once.
- **Languages.** Pick the languages you speak from tiles with their flags, with
  search. Automatic detection chooses among them only. Switch the language
  from the tray menu (Windows) or the menu bar (Mac).
  - Windows: twenty languages, including Arabic, Chinese, Hindi, Japanese,
    Korean, Turkish and Vietnamese.
  - Mac: the 25 European languages of the Parakeet model.
- **Windows look.** Telegram's night theme, refined: calmer cards, smoother
  buttons and switches, and pages that rise in quietly. Animations stay off
  when Windows animations are off.

## SuperDictate for Mac

A menu bar app made the Mac way. Press **Right ⌘**, speak, press it again, and
your words are typed where the cursor is. Speech is recognized on the Mac
itself, on the Apple Neural Engine, with NVIDIA Parakeet TDT v3; your voice is
never sent anywhere. A setup window walks you through the three permissions
and the one-time model download (about 460 MB).

The Mac app isn't notarized by Apple yet. The first time, right-click
SuperDictate in Applications, choose **Open**, then **Open** again. If macOS
still refuses, open **System Settings › Privacy & Security** and click **Open
Anyway**.

## Known limitations

- The Windows installer isn't signed with a certificate yet, so SmartScreen
  asks for confirmation: click **More info** › **Run anyway**.
- Windows: 64-bit Intel and AMD processors only. Mac: Apple Silicon only.
