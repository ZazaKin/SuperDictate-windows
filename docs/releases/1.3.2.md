# SuperDictate 1.3.2

Liquid Glass works on Windows 10.

Downloads:

- **Windows:** `SuperDictate-Setup-1.3.2.exe`. Installed copies offer the
  update on a click (**Settings › Support › Check for updates**).
- **Mac:** `SuperDictate-macOS-1.3.2.zip`, for Apple Silicon Macs with macOS 14
  Sonoma or newer. Nothing changed on the Mac since 1.3.1.

You can verify both files against `SHA256SUMS.txt`.

## Fixes

- **Windows 10: Liquid Glass shows the glass again.** The capsule was painted
  instead: Windows 10 can't keep a see-through window out of screen copies,
  which the live glass relied on. Now the glass copies the screen just before
  the capsule comes in and shows it blurred and bent at the edge, light or dark
  with what's behind it. If what's behind the capsule changes during a
  dictation, the glass catches up the next time the capsule comes in. On
  Windows 10 the capsule also appears in screenshots and screen sharing.

## Known limitations

- The Windows installer isn't signed with a certificate yet, so SmartScreen
  asks for confirmation: click **More info** › **Run anyway**.
- The Mac app isn't notarized by Apple yet. The first time, right-click
  SuperDictate in Applications, choose **Open**, then **Open** again.
- Windows: 64-bit Intel and AMD processors only. Mac: Apple Silicon only.
