# SuperDictate 1.2.0

Liquid Glass, as on an iPhone or a Mac, on both apps. Windows also catches up
with the Mac: the same 25 languages, Escape to cancel, and taps that don't
count as dictations.

Downloads:

- **Windows:** `SuperDictate-Setup-1.2.0.exe`. Installed copies offer the
  update on a click (**Settings › Support › Check for updates**).
- **Mac:** `SuperDictate-macOS-1.2.0.zip`, for Apple Silicon Macs with macOS 14
  Sonoma or newer.

You can verify both files against `SHA256SUMS.txt`.

## What's new

- **Liquid Glass.** A new capsule skin, now one of thirteen.
  - **Mac:** on macOS 26 it's Apple's own glass, and the default skin. On
    macOS 14 and 15 it's a close recreation.
  - **Windows:** the screen behind the capsule shows through it, softly
    blurred and bent at the edge, with light catching the rim. Over a bright
    window the glass turns light with dark words; over a dark one it stays
    dark with white words.
- **Widest.** A new slider on the Capsule page sets how far the capsule grows
  as your words arrive.
- **Windows, now as on the Mac:**
  - **Escape** cancels a dictation and nothing is typed. With press and hold,
    typing another key while you hold the hotkey cancels too.
  - A press too short to be speech, under a third of a second, is ignored.
  - The same 25 European languages as the Mac, each with its flag. If you had
    picked Turkish, Arabic, Hindi, Chinese, Japanese, Korean or Vietnamese,
    automatic detection now chooses among your other languages, or English if
    none are left.

## Good to know

- With **Live glass** on, which is the default for Liquid Glass on Windows,
  the capsule doesn't appear in screenshots or screen sharing: to show the
  real screen behind it, it keeps itself out of screen copies. Turn **Live
  glass** off on the Capsule page for a painted Liquid Glass that does appear.
  Live glass needs Windows 10 version 2004 or later.

## Known limitations

- The Windows installer isn't signed with a certificate yet, so SmartScreen
  asks for confirmation: click **More info** › **Run anyway**.
- The Mac app isn't notarized by Apple yet. The first time, right-click
  SuperDictate in Applications, choose **Open**, then **Open** again.
- Windows: 64-bit Intel and AMD processors only. Mac: Apple Silicon only.
