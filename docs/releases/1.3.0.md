# SuperDictate 1.3.0

Fifteen capsule skins, seven of them alive, two new voice meters, and settings
on Windows that wait for Save.

Downloads:

- **Windows:** `SuperDictate-Setup-1.3.0.exe`. Installed copies offer the
  update on a click (**Settings › Support › Check for updates**).
- **Mac:** `SuperDictate-macOS-1.3.0.zip`, for Apple Silicon Macs with macOS 14
  Sonoma or newer.

You can verify both files against `SHA256SUMS.txt`.

## What's new

- **Fifteen skins, seven with moving art.** A picture moves under the words and
  comes alive as you speak, the same on Windows and the Mac:
  - **Bloom:** grainy pastel light pools that drift and swell.
  - **Eclipse:** a planet's lit edge; the glow rises with your voice.
  - **Chrome:** dark ribbons with iridescent edges and a sliding glint.
  - **Hive:** a honeycomb lit by a wandering violet light.
  - **Halftone:** a pink pixel wave that climbs as you speak.
  - **Mosaic:** lavender-to-pink tiles with a shimmer running through them.
  - **Mesa:** clouds drifting over terracotta hills.

  The art calms behind the line of words, and the words get a soft halo, so
  they always read. With animations off on Windows, or Reduce Motion on the
  Mac, the picture holds still.
- **New voice meters: Dots and Scope.** Dots is a small LED matrix; Scope is an
  oscilloscope line. They replace Pulse, whose circle could be cut off at the
  top as it swelled. No meter draws outside its space any more.
- **Windows: settings wait for Save.** Changes stay in the window until you
  click **Save**, which is greyed out until something changes. Save writes only
  what you changed in the window, so a change made meanwhile from the tray
  stays. Leaving a page or closing the window with unsaved changes asks:
  **Save**, **Don't save** or **Keep editing**. Problems such as a duplicate
  shortcut show when you save.
- **While the Capsule settings are open, dictation waits.** The capsule on
  screen is the sample you're editing, so pressing the shortcut shows "Close
  Capsule settings to dictate" instead of starting to record. On both apps.
- **Mac:** the recording time no longer wraps onto two lines next to a long
  line of words.

## Good to know

- Ocean, Sunset, Forest, Frost and Telegram are gone. If you used one of them,
  or the Pulse meter, the capsule switches to Midnight or Bars; pick a new look
  on the Capsule page.

## Known limitations

- The Windows installer isn't signed with a certificate yet, so SmartScreen
  asks for confirmation: click **More info** › **Run anyway**.
- The Mac app isn't notarized by Apple yet. The first time, right-click
  SuperDictate in Applications, choose **Open**, then **Open** again.
- Windows: 64-bit Intel and AMD processors only. Mac: Apple Silicon only.
